About Me

Who I Am

I am a results-oriented Civil Engineering Technician with over six years of experience spanning construction site supervision, CAD and BIM production, and project coordination across Uganda and South Sudan.

My work sits at the intersection of technical drawing precision and on-the-ground construction knowledge — I don't just draw buildings, I understand how they are built. This gives my CAD and BIM outputs a practical accuracy that purely office-based technicians often lack.

I am currently offering CAD and BIM management services on a freelance basis to Architecture, Engineering, and Construction (AEC) clients, while pursuing a Certified Associate in Project Management (CAPM) certification from the Project Management Institute.
